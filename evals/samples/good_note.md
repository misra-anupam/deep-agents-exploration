# ACME FY2024: Neutral

Growth is real, but earnings are not converting to cash. Stance: Neutral.

## Results vs. prior year
- Revenue rose 12.0% to $1,008.0 million [XBRL Revenues FY2024, accn 0009999999-25-000001].
- Net income rose 17.7% to $113.0 million [XBRL NetIncomeLoss FY2024, accn 0009999999-25-000001].
- Operating cash flow was $87.0 million versus $88.0 million [XBRL NetCashProvidedByUsedInOperatingActivities FY2024, accn 0009999999-25-000001].

## Quality of earnings
- Receivables grew 34.8% to $182.0 million, well ahead of 12.0% revenue growth [XBRL AccountsReceivableNetCurrent FY2024, accn 0009999999-25-000001].
- DSO rose from 54.8 to 65.9 days as payment terms for certain distributors were extended from 45 to 75 days [10-K 0009999999-25-000001, Item 1A].
- Cash conversion (operating cash flow / net income) fell to 0.77x, below our 0.9x floor [XBRL NetCashProvidedByUsedInOperatingActivities FY2024, accn 0009999999-25-000001].

## Risks
- The three largest distributors were 41% of revenue, up from 33% [10-K 0009999999-25-000001, Item 1A].

## What would change our mind
- DSO returning below 55 days, or cash conversion back above 0.9x.
