ACME INDUSTRIAL CORP (SYNTHETIC - FICTIONAL COMPANY FOR TESTING)
ANNUAL REPORT ON FORM 10-K FOR THE FISCAL YEAR ENDED DECEMBER 31, 2024

Item 1. Business

ACME Industrial Corp designs and sells industrial fastening systems to
construction and manufacturing customers. We operate two segments: Distribution
(approximately 70% of revenue) and Direct Sales (approximately 30% of revenue).

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Customer concentration. Our three largest distributors accounted for 41% of
fiscal 2024 revenue, up from 33% in fiscal 2023. The loss of any of these
distributors could materially affect results.

Extended payment terms. To support growth in Distribution, we extended standard
payment terms for certain distributors from 45 days to 75 days during fiscal
2024. Longer terms increase our working capital needs and credit risk.

Raw materials. Steel represents roughly 38% of our cost of revenue. Price
volatility may affect margins.

Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis

Overview. Revenue grew 12.0% to $1,008.0 million in fiscal 2024 from $900.0
million in fiscal 2023, driven by volume growth in Distribution. Gross margin
was 41.0%, consistent with the prior year. Net income increased 17.7% to $113.0
million.

Liquidity. Net cash provided by operating activities was $87.0 million in
fiscal 2024 compared with $88.0 million in fiscal 2023. The decrease was
primarily due to an increase in accounts receivable, which rose to $182.0
million from $135.0 million, reflecting higher sales to distributors in the
fourth quarter and the extended payment terms described under Item 1A.

Capital expenditures were $40.0 million, including $9.0 million for a new
fulfillment facility. We expect fiscal 2025 capital expenditures of $45 to $50
million.

Outlook. We expect fiscal 2025 revenue growth of 8% to 10%.

Item 8. Notes to Consolidated Financial Statements

Note 4. Accounts Receivable. Accounts receivable are stated net of an allowance
for credit losses of $3.1 million (2023: $3.0 million). Receivables from our
three largest distributors totaled $88.0 million at December 31, 2024.

Note 9. Revenue Recognition. Revenue from Distribution is recognized upon
shipment. In the fourth quarter of fiscal 2024, shipments to distributors
totaled $312.0 million, compared with $258.0 million in the fourth quarter of
fiscal 2023.
